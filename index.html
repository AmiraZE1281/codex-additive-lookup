Important regulatory note: This tool is a searchable reference aid based on the supplied Codex source documents and the Codex Additive Search Database. A “No, No GSFA provision found” result means that no mapped GSFA provision was found in the current database. It must not be interpreted as a finding that an additive is unsafe or prohibited. Verify the applicable Codex text and relevant national regulatory requirements before making a regulatory decision. Inclusion of an additive in the INS should not by itself be interpreted as Codex approval for use.
